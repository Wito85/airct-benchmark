## 1. Provenance and disclosure

1.1 On 17 September 2026 the senior author consulted the Institut für Medizinische Informatik und Biometrie (IMB), TU Dresden (Prof. Dr. Ingo Röder, Director; Matthias Kuhn, lead statistician) on the statistical analysis plan v1.0. Two recommendations were given and confirmed in writing on 6 October 2026: (a) the dataset is a complete enumeration of PubMed records at the retrieval date and should be described as such, without significance tests; (b) the comparison should be made within fields (field with AI versus field without AI) rather than between the AI field and disease fields. The reason for (b) is interpretability. Within one disease, the records with and without the AI heading share the disease, its baseline propensity to randomized trials and its indexing practice, and differ in AI involvement only, so that a difference in the RCT share can be read as a difference between AI-related and other research on the same disease. In the field-level contrast of v2.0 the two fields differ in disease, in trial propensity and in size, and the AI set overlaps with every disease set, so that no comparison of like with like stands behind the ratio.

1.2 The authors accept both recommendations. The justification for (a) follows Behnke (Politische Vierteljahresschrift 2005;46(1):O1 to O15) and Schneider (Journal of Informetrics 2013;7(1):50 to 62): inferential statistics on a complete enumeration are justified only if the data are treated as one realization of a specified random process; the research goal of this study is to describe the indexed literature as it is, not to ask whether it could have arisen from such a process, and a significance test on a bibliometric population answers a question that is not asked. The one random element that remains is measurement: the RCT publication type is an imperfect measurement of RCT status, and its error is estimated from validation cells that are random samples of the frozen lists. Uncertainty is therefore quantified for measurement (validation proportions and the quantities derived from them, as measurement uncertainty intervals, Section 2.3) and reported in words for two named diagnostics: indexing lag by E7 (the proportion of MEDLINE-indexed records per year) and within-day volatility by E11 (Run B minus Run A per cell, handled by the rule of D26).

1.3 Recommendation (b) does not change the data source, the field definitions, the human-subject base (D12), the study window (D13), the freeze protocol (D18) or the validation design; it changes the primary contrast and adds query families. For the nine registered comparators the within-field contrast uses the direct queries of the registered families D and E and of the new family DB (Section 4); the registered family B enters the consistency check E16 only.

1.4 Pre-freeze checks for the amendment follow the rule of Q8 and Q18: publication year 2015 only, every request logged and disclosed verbatim in the Q18 addendum (Section 8.10). The check is one run of the pipeline command syntax-check --send, identified in the addendum by its git commit and the SHA-256 of its source files. It sends 33 requests in three groups. (a) Eleven requests for the new compositions of families DV, QV, WV, KV and RS that are not trial numerators, each with Diabetes Mellitus, the registered smoke field, in place of Stroke, so that no Stroke count is viewed. (b) One request for each of the 18 GBD query expressions that are new relative to v2.0 (family GA, human-subject denominator). (c) Four registered smoke cells of Diabetes Mellitus 2015 that were disclosed in Q18 of v2.0 (denominators of families A, E and K and the enriched untagged stratum of family R); they are read again on the same day, because the database is live and the partition identities of the check need values of one day. Not run: every composition that is a trial numerator within a field (families DB, DC and JV and each variant with the Randomized Controlled Trial publication type); these add a registered publication-type clause, verified on 4 September 2026 (Q18), to a composition that is checked here or registered. Families MD, ME, GD and GE apply the registered compositions of families D and E to registered headings or to the expressions checked under (b). No Stroke count, no 2024 count, no within-field RCT count and no count of any GBD descriptor for any year other than 2015 is viewed before the freeze run.

1.5 In a second exchange (September 2026) the IMB confirmed the scope of its support (review of the amended plan, help with the justification of the census framework, advice on small cells) and stated the distinction that governs D21 and D23: in a complete enumeration small counts are simply small counts and can only be described, whereas a cell that is a sample of a larger set carries sampling uncertainty.

1.6 On 6 October 2026 the IMB (M. Kuhn, I. Röder) returned its written review of the summary of the amended analysis plan of 30 September 2026. The review confirmed the design and the census framework and raised points of wording, notation and analysis detail, each of which is worked into this version: the justification of the primary contrast (1.1, 8.2); the literature (1.2, 2.5); the diagnostics named by their labels (1.2); a section on definitions and notation with exactly three AI definitions (1A, 2.4, 8.8); the symbols k, m, C and n (1A, 6.4, 6.5); the name "posterior simulation" and the Jeffreys prior with a sensitivity to the prior (6.5, 8.7); the sampled-or-complete rule written out per validation cell (6.1, 8.7); the separate reporting of kappa (6.3); the direct query DB as the only analysis value of the overlap numerator (4, 8.5); the wording "least-squares slope of log PR_within on calendar year" (3, 7, 8.5); the trend in two layers with a prespecified Loess smoother (8.5); the permutation position of the slope as the exploratory endpoint E17 (3, 8.12); and the two-panel figure of the stroke series (9). On the questions the review left to the authors, five decisions were taken by the guarantor on 6 October 2026: (1) the flag of D21 applies below 10 RCT-tagged records in the numerator instead of below 5; (2) a third pooled window 2015 to 2019 is added; (3) the Loess smoothing is part of S1, and the permutation is exploratory only, without threshold; (4) a difference between Run A and Run B is handled by the three-tier rule D26 with Run A as the analysis value; (5) pooled windows are sums of the yearly cells, with the disclosure of D27. ## 1A. Definitions and notation

Every term is defined once here and used with this meaning throughout the amendment, the SAP v2.0 and the manuscript.

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Record | One PubMed record, identified by its PMID. |
| Cell | The count returned by one query string for one field, one metric and one publication year: a complete enumeration of the records that match the string at the retrieval date. Where a PMID list is drawn (families L and LV), the list of the same query. |
| Field | A set of records defined by a query expression: a named field of Q12.3, a heading of the disease-category panel of Q12.5, or a GBD panel member of Section 5.7. All fields are taken on the human-subject base ("Humans"[Mesh]) unless a record base is named. |
| Named field | A field with a registered key of Q12.3: AI, STROKE, DM, MI, CVD, COVID, MOBAPP, CDSS, DXIMG, TELE. The nine named fields other than AI are the comparators. |
| AI heading | "Artificial Intelligence"[Mesh], exploded: AI definition 1 of the table below. |
| AI subset, complement | For a field F: the AI subset is F AND the AI heading, the complement is F NOT the AI heading, both on the same record base and in the same publication year. The two partition the field. |
| Within-field | A quantity computed for the AI subset and the complement of the same field, same base, same year. |
| Tag | The publication type Randomized Controlled Trial as assigned by the NLM, queried as "Randomized Controlled Trial"[pt]. A record that carries it is RCT-tagged. The tag is the measurement; RCT status is the quantity measured. The Clinical Trial tag ("Clinical Trial"[pt]) is used in S4 only. |
| RCT share | p = a / n: the RCT-tagged records of a cell divided by all its records. |
| Within-field prevalence ratio | PR_within = p_AI / p_nonAI, the RCT share of the AI subset divided by the RCT share of the complement. Fold difference: 1 / PR_within. Absolute difference: p_AI minus p_nonAI in percentage points. |
| AI penetration | n_AI / n_F: the records of the AI subset divided by all records of the field. |
| Complete enumeration (census) | Every record is counted; no selection takes place, so no sampling uncertainty exists and no interval is attached. Every cell is a complete enumeration. |
| Validation cell | A set of records rated by hand against the codebook of Q12.7: V-cells (RCT-tagged records), U-cells (enriched untagged stratum, family R or RS) and the R-check (remainder stratum). |
| Sampled cell | A validation cell whose source list holds more than 200 records; 200 of them are rated, chosen by the seeded sequence. It carries sampling uncertainty, which is quantified. |
| Completely rated cell | A validation cell whose source list holds 200 records or fewer; every record is rated. Its proportion is exact and carries no interval. |
| Seeded sequence, seed | One call random.Random(20260904).sample(list, k = length of the list) on the PMID list sorted ascending (decision I1 of v1.0). The seed 20260904 is used for every random draw of the study, including the posterior simulation. |
| Pilot | The first 20 records of the seeded sequence of a sampled cell, rated jointly to calibrate the codebook and not counted in the formal set. A completely rated cell has no pilot of its own. |
| Flag | The mark of decision D21 on a cell whose RCT-tagged count is below 10 (tag-error sensitive), and on every ratio into which such a cell enters. |
| Pooled window | The sum of the yearly cells of the years named, numerator and denominator separately (D27). Windows: 2015 to 2019, 2020 to 2024, 2015 to 2024. |
| Run A, Run B | The two complete passes of the catalogue in the freeze session (D18). Run A is the analysis dataset; Run B is the within-day repeat (E11), handled by D26. |
| Posterior simulation | The procedure of Section 6.5: 10,000 draws of each sampled validation proportion from its Beta posterior, propagated through the formulas of S5 and S7. Called bootstrap in v2.0 and in the draft of 30 September 2026. |
| Prior | The Beta distribution combined with the rated records of a sampled cell to form its posterior. Used: the Jeffreys prior Beta(0.5, 0.5); sensitivity: Beta(1, 1) and Beta(1/3, 1/3). |
| Measurement uncertainty interval | The 2.5th to 97.5th percentile range of a posterior simulation. It expresses uncertainty about the measurement (the proportions of the sampled validation cells), not sampling uncertainty of the literature, and is the only interval attached to any count-derived quantity. |
| Slope | The least-squares slope of log PR_within on calendar year, fitted to the yearly values; exp(slope) is the annual multiplicative change. A descriptive summary without standard error. |
| Loess | Locally weighted least-squares smoothing of a yearly share on calendar year: locally linear, span 0.75, tricube weights, no robustness iterations. Descriptive. |
| Permutation position | The proportion of permutations of the year labels whose slope is at least as large in absolute value as the observed slope (E17). A description of the series at hand, not a test. |
| Query expression | The string that defines a field, a heading or a GBD panel member, used verbatim in every composition. For a GBD member: one exploded descriptor, or one descriptor with another removed in the form of 5.3. |
| Shared cell | A cell of a GBD member that coincides with a named field (Stroke, Diabetes Mellitus); its count is read from the registered cell and the string is not requested again. |

Notation. Symbols of a literature cell: n, all records of the cell (the denominator); a, its RCT-tagged records (the numerator); p = a / n; size(S), the number of records of the enriched untagged stratum S of the cell (families R and RS); subscripts AI and nonAI for the AI subset and the complement of a field, and F and y for field and year where needed. Symbols of a validation cell: m, the number of rated records; k, the number of them with the property in question after adjudication; k / m, the validation proportion. k and m belong to the hand-rated validation cell; they are not a and n, which belong to the whole literature cell. Estimands from validation cells: pi_true, the proportion of true RCT reports among RCT-tagged records (V-cell); pi_primary, the proportion of primary reports among them; pi_missed, the proportion of RCT reports among the records of the enriched untagged stratum (U-cell); pi_missed_primary, the same for primary reports; pi_intervention, the proportion of records in which AI is the intervention (V3 = ai_intervention) among the true RCT reports of an AI-related V-cell; pi_field, the proportion of records with stroke as the target condition (V5 = target) in a STROKE cell. Derived quantities of a literature cell: C = a x pi_true + size(S) x pi_missed, the tag-corrected numerator, with p_corr = C / n (S7); p_adj = p x pi_true x pi_intervention x pi_field for the AI subset and p x pi_true x pi_field for the complement (S5); PR_corr and PR_adj, the ratios of the corrected and the adjusted shares. Family letters (A, B, D, E, DB, ...) stand for the count of that family's cell where a formula names them, for example PR_within = (DB / D) / (E_RCT / E_den).

## 2. Change 1: statistical framework (D25)

2.1 Count cells are complete enumerations at the retrieval date. Every count-based quantity (share, prevalence ratio, absolute difference, penetration, growth rate, slope, rank) is reported as an exact population quantity of the frozen dataset. No p-value, no confidence interval, no standard error and no significance language is attached to any count-based quantity.

2.2 The prespecified expectation for the primary endpoint (PR_within below 1) is a directional statement about a population quantity. It is either met or not met in the frozen dataset; this is reported as a fact, not as a test result, and no redefinition follows if it is not met.

2.3 Intervals are reported only for quantities with a random element: (a) validation proportions of sampled cells (Wilson 95% intervals, as registered), because those cells are simple random samples of the frozen PMID lists; a completely rated cell has an exact proportion and no interval; (b) validation-adjusted and tag-corrected quantities (S5, S7), by posterior simulation over the proportions of the sampled validation cells only (D23), with completely rated cells entering as exact proportions; the central 95 percent of the simulated values is the measurement uncertainty interval, which expresses uncertainty about the measurement and not sampling uncertainty of the literature; (c) inter-rater kappa (asymptotic 95% interval, as registered), reported separately per cell as a description of agreement; kappa does not enter any interval of (b), the adjudicated values do.

2.4 The trend analysis S1 is descriptive in two layers (Section 8.5): the yearly shares smoothed by a prespecified Loess smoother with the ratio of the smoothed shares, and the least-squares slope of log PR_within on calendar year. The quasi-Poisson model, the negative binomial sensitivity, the factor-year and spline checks and all model-based intervals are retired. The agreement rule D19 is retained as a robustness rule: a direction of change is stated only if the sign of the slope agrees across the three AI definitions of Section 1A.

2.5 Manuscript consequence: the Methods section contains a paragraph justifying the absence of tests in a complete enumeration, citing Behnke (2005), Berk, Western and Weiss (Sociological Methodology 1995;25:421 to 458) and Schneider (Journal of Informetrics 2013;7(1):50 to 62, DOI 10.1016/j.joi.2012.08.005), reviewed by the IMB within the consultation. Broscheid and Gschwend (2003), cited in the draft of 30 September 2026, is no longer cited.

6.4 Estimands, each the proportion k / m of its validation cell (Section 1A): pi_true and pi_primary in the V-cells; pi_missed and pi_missed_primary in the U-cells; pi_intervention in the AI-related V-cells; pi_field in the STROKE cells. The former field-level estimands for AI and STROKE are obtained from the union of the split cells, weighted by the frozen cell sizes.

6.5 Posterior simulation (D23): 10,000 draws, seed 20260904. For every sampled validation cell (more than 200 records in its list, 200 rated), the proportion is drawn from the Beta posterior Beta(k + 0.5, m minus k + 0.5), that is the posterior under the Jeffreys prior Beta(0.5, 0.5) with k adjudicated successes among m rated records. The Jeffreys prior is chosen because it is the reference prior for a binomial proportion, invariant to reparameterization, and its equal-tailed posterior interval is one of the intervals recommended for a binomial proportion on the strength of its coverage (Brown, Cai and DasGupta, Statistical Science 2001;16(2):101 to 133); with m = 200 it moves the posterior mean by less than 0.0025 away from k / m. For every completely rated cell (200 records or fewer, all rated), the adjudicated proportion enters as an exact value without a draw. Tagged counts, stratum sizes and denominators are fixed at their frozen values. Each draw is propagated through the formulas of S5 and S7; the 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles of the 10,000 results form the measurement uncertainty interval, which expresses uncertainty from the sampled validation cells only. A quantity whose validation inputs are all complete is reported without an interval. Sensitivity to the prior: the simulation is repeated with Beta(1, 1) (uniform prior) and Beta(1/3, 1/3), and the three intervals are reported side by side; the interval under the Jeffreys prior is the reported one. The procedure was called bootstrap in v2.0 and in the draft of 30 September 2026; it is a posterior simulation and is named so throughout.

## 7. Reporting rules: small cells (D21), pooled windows (D27), Run A versus Run B (D26)

7.1 Small cells (D21). Nothing is suppressed. Every count and every ratio, within-field or field-level, for every year and every pooled window, is reported with its four counts. A cell whose RCT-tagged count is below 10 is flagged as tag-error sensitive in every table and figure, because a single misclassified record changes such a count by more than 10 percent; the flag marks measurement sensitivity and makes no statement about sampling, since in a complete enumeration small counts are what they are and can only be described. The threshold refers to the numerator, the RCT-tagged count; a ratio is flagged if either of its numerators is flagged. Summaries are given twice, over all fields (or years) and over the unflagged subset, with the number of flagged cells stated. In every figure the plotting symbol of a share or a ratio is scaled by the smallest count that enters the value, and flagged values are drawn with an open symbol. The slope of S1 is computed over all years in which both cells have a non-zero RCT-tagged count and, in addition, over the unflagged years; the number of years used is stated in each case. The primary endpoint P is reported in every case; if the 2024 STROKE AND AI cell is flagged, the abstract reports the 2024 counts together with the pooled 2020 to 2024 contrast (S1b). No Haldane correction and no Koopman interval are used; a zero count leaves the ratio undefined, which is shown as such. The threshold is 10 instead of the 5 of the draft of 30 September 2026 (decision 1 of 6 October 2026); 10 is also the count below which v2.0 added the Koopman interval.

7.2 Pooled windows (D27). A pooled window (2015 to 2019, 2020 to 2024, 2015 to 2024) is formed by summing the yearly cells of the window, numerator and denominator separately; no query of its own is made for a window, so that the catalogue stays as registered and as extended in Section 4. Disclosure: the [pdat] field of PubMed matches the electronic and the print publication date of a record. A record whose two dates fall in different calendar years is a member of both yearly cells and is therefore counted twice in a pooled sum, in the numerator and the denominator alike; a pooled share is a count-weighted average of the yearly shares and not the share of distinct records. The size of this double counting cannot be measured from counts and is stated as a limitation. A window query of its own, which would count each record once, is not part of this amendment because it would require a change of the frozen pipeline before the freeze run.

7.3 Run A versus Run B (D26). Run A is the analysis dataset, as registered. For every cell, Run B minus Run A is reported (E11) and handled in three tiers. Tier 1, the two counts are equal: nothing further. Tier 2, the absolute difference is at most 1 record or at most 1 percent of the Run A count, whichever is larger: Run A stays the analysis value, and every quantity that uses the cell is shown with its Run B value in addition in the supplement. Tier 3, a larger difference: the cause is investigated on the freeze day (service messages, attempts, the logs of both runs, indexing activity of the day) and recorded; if the cause remains unexplained, Run A and Run B are repeated as a whole on Monday 26 October 2026, and the first attempt is reported next to the repetition with the differing cells named. The decision to repeat is taken by the guarantor on the freeze day and recorded in the reproduction log on OSF. The tiers apply to every cell of the catalogue, registered and added; nothing is averaged across the runs. (Decision 4 of 6 October 2026, on the question of the IMB whether differing cells should be reported only or should enter the flag; the IMB also regarded the rounded mean of the two runs as acceptable, which is not used.)
